<objective>
Create the extensible content-category framework: a registry module at `src/lib/ai/categories/` with one module per category, plus News and Facts & Info headline-first prompt modules. This is the copy engine that Wave 2 generation wiring selects by `category` instead of by `templateId`.

Purpose: Today `templateId` is the only style signal Gemini receives. Phase 82 decouples *what kind of content* (category) from *how it looks* (template). The category drives the copy. This plan builds the registry (mirroring `src/lib/render/templates/index.ts`) and the two shipping prompt modules — News and Facts — each instructing one big bold headline per slide, accent-highlighted keywords via `hook_segments`, omitted body text, and optional sparse accessory labels. Health & Wellness and Beauty are framework-only this phase (not built here).

Output: `src/lib/ai/categories/` with `types.ts` (the `CategoryModule` interface), `news.ts`, `facts.ts`, `index.ts` (the registry: `CATEGORIES`, `CONTENT_CATEGORIES`, `getCategory()`, the `ContentCategory` union type), and a registry unit test.
</objective>
