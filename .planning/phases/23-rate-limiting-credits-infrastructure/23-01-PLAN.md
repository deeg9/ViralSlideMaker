| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-23-01 | Denial of Service | rate-limit.ts | mitigate | Upstash sliding window at 60 req/min per api_key_id; 429 with Retry-After |
| T-23-02 | Spoofing | cors.ts | accept | Wildcard CORS origin is intentional for public Bearer-token API; no cookies involved |
| T-23-03 | Information Disclosure | errors.ts | mitigate | Error messages are generic (no stack traces, no internal details); structured codes only |
