    1. On mount (useEffect), call `generateImagePromptAction(topic, slide.hook_text, slide.body_text, slide.is_cta)` to auto-populate prompt. Show skeleton pulse in textarea while loading (`animate-pulse bg-[#fff0f0] rounded h-20`). Textarea `aria-label="Image generation prompt"`, `aria-busy="true"` during loading.     2. **Prompt ready state (IMAGEN-04):** Show editable textarea (4 rows desktop, 3 rows mobile) with auto-generated prompt. Below: credit cost badge `<span className="text-xs bg-[#fadbdb] text-[#3d0000] px-2 py-0.5 rounded-full" aria-label="This action costs 1 credit">1 credit</span>` and "Generate Image" button: `bg-[#3d0000] text-white rounded-xl px-4 py-2.5 text-sm font-bold`. Button has `aria-label="Generate AI background image, costs 1 credit"`.
    3. **Generating state:** Use `useTransition` for the generate action. Button disabled immediately on click (double-click prevention per research pitfall 5). Show spinner icon `progress_activity` with `animate-spin`. Text below: "Generating image... this may take up to 15 seconds." Textarea disabled.
    4. **Success state:** Call `onUpdate({ background_url: result.url, pexels_photo_id: null })` to apply image to slide. Show small success message briefly. Prompt textarea remains for re-generation.
    5. **Safety filtered (refunded):** Show inline warning with `role="alert"`: amber/muted bg `bg-[#fff0f0]`, Icon `warning`, text: "Image was blocked by content safety filter. Your credit has been refunded. Try a different description." Textarea remains editable.
    6. **Insufficient credits:** Show inline message: "Not enough credits. [Buy more credits](/settings/subscription)" in `text-[#7d6161]` with link.
    7. **API error (refunded):** Show inline with `role="alert"`: "Generation failed. Your credit has been refunded. Please try again."
