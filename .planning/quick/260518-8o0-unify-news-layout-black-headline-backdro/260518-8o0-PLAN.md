<objective>
On news-layout slides the solid-black region behind the headline is currently the
slide root `backgroundColor` showing below a top-constrained news photo. It is NOT
the Caption card, so the editor's "Caption card" toggle reads OFF even though a
black block is plainly visible. This plan unifies the two: the news-layout headline
backdrop BECOMES a caption card driven by the existing `resolveCaptionBar` /
`caption_bar_*` system.

Purpose: One mechanism ("a colored block behind the caption") on every slide,
including news-layout slides — so the toggle reflects reality and the caption-card
controls (color, opacity, snap, drag/resize) actually edit the block.

Output:
- `resolveCaptionBar` gains a news-layout-aware default: an unedited news slide
  resolves `enabled: true` with seeded bottom-region black geometry (looks
  identical to today on first load).
- News-layout photo becomes full-bleed in all 3 render paths.
- Editor toggle reads the resolved state (ON for unedited news slides) and writes
  an explicit `caption_bar_enabled: false` on OFF (so OFF survives the resolver
  default).
- The dead `news_image_height` resolver wiring is retired; `background_color` is
  kept (still the raw slide background revealed when the card is OFF and no photo
  exists).
</objective>
