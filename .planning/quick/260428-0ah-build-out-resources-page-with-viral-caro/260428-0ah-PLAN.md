## Web research highlights (2026)

- Carousels get **~1.9× more reach** than single images; **8–10 slides** is the engagement sweet spot. Engagement drops at slide 3 and rebounds at slide 8+ (Instagram "second chance" re-serve at slide 2).
- **First slide gets ~2 seconds** to earn a swipe. Strong hooks → 60–80% swipe-through; weak → ~20%.
- **Curiosity gap** is the core hook mechanic (Loewenstein information-gap theory). Question / bold claim / surprising stat / promise of transformation.
- **Text legibility:** sans-serif, ≥24px body / ≥36px headline, WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1), 10–15 words/slide max, 1 idea per slide.
- **CTA on last slide** lifts saves/shares/comments — "Save this for later", "Tag someone who needs this", "Comment X to get the link", "Follow for more".
- TikTok carousels: even punchier — 15–25 words/slide, more visual, up to 15 slides.
